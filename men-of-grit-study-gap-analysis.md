3. **A "Logos Library" page** sits under the Hub. Not opened — it appeared to be a resource index rather than a study, so it's excluded from the coverage baseline.

4. **The 7 Community Member profile pages** (under Community Members) were not individually opened. They're described as auto-populated daily Slack post logs. **Members' own reflections could contain Bible references I have not captured.** If you want those included, that's a separate pass over 7 pages.

5. **Reference counts are approximate.** I counted appearances of a passage across week overviews, key-verse lists, prose, daily readings, and Jon's own handwritten reflections. Where a passage appears twice on one page (e.g. in the key-verse list and again in the Mindshift prose), I generally counted it twice. Treat the counts as relative weight, not exact tallies.

6. **Chapter-only daily readings** (e.g. "Daily Bible Reading: Romans 12") are counted as references to the whole chapter. Where a reading was verse-scoped (e.g. "Romans 8:18–30"), I recorded the scope given.

7. **Nothing was invented.** Every passage listed above was read directly off a page in the tree. Where a reference appeared only inside an existing study (e.g. Micah 6:8 and Genesis 5:24 in the 2 John word study), I excluded it — those are not Men of Grit references.
